## Post 3: light recipes

Image: `posts/post-3-light-recipes-en.jpg` / `-ar.jpg`

### English

Your LEDs are probably your biggest power bill. Do you know what each light recipe costs you per kilo?

In Lamina you can switch a floor between red + blue, full white and blue-heavy light, and watch growth speed and energy per kilo change before you touch a real fixture.

On the demo's sample farm, switching basil from red + blue to blue-heavy drops growth speed to 76%. That's the kind of trade-off you'd want to see before the electricity bill does.

Try it on floor 2:

Five steps, usually tracked in five different places. In Lamina they're one strip at the bottom of the screen, with a live log of what happened and when: which floor was harvested, how many crates were packed, when the van left.

Grow managers see what's ready. Operations sees what's leaving. Owners see all of it.
