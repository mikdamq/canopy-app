# Demo video scripts: one per farm type

Written 20 Oct 2026. Short screen recordings of the live demo, **30 to 60 seconds**, one for each kind of farm. Use them:

- as the reply when someone asks "what does it look like for a farm like mine?" on WhatsApp or LinkedIn;
- as LinkedIn video posts and Instagram Reels;
- on the site's solution pages later.

They're general, so record each once and reuse it. The **personal** video for a farm that sent a request is a different routine (60–90 s, their farm's name): `docs/launch-kit/demo-video.md`.

The Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic, for Mikdam to check before recording.

## Recording setup (all four)

- **Link:** `https://laminafarm.app/<en|ar>/demo?farm=Green%20Valley&type=<tower|container|greenhouse|lab>` (Arabic: farm name `مزرعة الوادي`).
- **Window:** 1440 × 900 for LinkedIn and WhatsApp. For a Reel, use your phone held upright, or a narrow browser window (about 540 × 960), then export 1080 × 1920.
- **Before recording:** close the welcome card and skip the tour. Set the clock to about 15:00, press Play.
- **Tool:** the macOS screen recorder (Cmd + Shift + 5) or the Windows Snipping Tool. Voice optional: every script works muted with the on-screen text added in editing.
- **Pace:** slow mouse moves; let each change sit for 2 seconds.
- **End card (3 s):** the logo, and "Try it with your farm's name · laminafarm.app" / "جرّبها باسم مزرعتك · laminafarm.app".

Each script: what to do on screen, the on-screen text (short, both languages), and the voice-over.

> This is a vertical farm as a live 3D twin. Every floor, every crop, in one view. Click a floor and you see its air, CO₂, water and lights, right where the sensors are.
