Canopy UX Review

I used the site the way a farm owner would: from a shared link to the landing page, into the personalised demo, through every control, and on to sending a pilot request. Then I did it all again in Arabic and on a phone. This page lists what to fix, in the order I'd fix it, and how the flow changes now that there are no calls.

Summary

A strong demo inside a flow that still expects a phone call

The personalised demo is the best part of the product. The farm's name is everywhere, every control changes something real, and the Arabic version feels native rather than translated. Nothing crashed in more than 40 screens, and accessibility scores 100 on every page.

The weak spots are at the edges of the demo. Getting in requires typing a name, and getting out leads nowhere except the request form. On phones, the request form loses everything on Back and makes you scroll before every step. Most importantly, the whole story still ends in "book a 30-minute call", which no longer matches how you want to run this.

  • Light recipes change the numbers: Blue heavy drops growth speed from 88% to 67% and raises energy from 9.4 to 10.7 kWh/kg.
  • Harvesting writes to the seed-to-plate log, and night mode lights the tower pink.
  • The form explains every error in both languages and moves focus to the first problem. "1,200 m²" is understood.
  • Odd input is safe: HTML and emoji in a farm name are shown as plain text.

No-call path

From demo to pilot without a single call

Today every route ends in a 30-minute call. Without calls, the site has to do three jobs the call used to do: answer questions, collect what you need to build the twin, and tell you whether the farm would really use it. Here's how each step changes.

Today

  1. LandingType a farm name to open the demo.
  2. DemoExplore, then "Get this for my farm".
  3. Request3 steps: you, your farm, your goals.
  4. Book a callCal.com calendar, or "we'll email you to set a time".
  5. 30-minute callQuestions, demo walkthrough, pilot offer.
  6. SetupTwin built in about two weeks.

Proposed

  1. LandingName optional: "or explore a sample farm".
  2. DemoPick your farm type (tower, container, greenhouse). A "try this" checklist leads to "Get this for my farm".
  3. RequestSame 3 steps. The last one adds an optional upload: a photo or sketch of your layout.
  4. What's next"Send us photos on WhatsApp" (message ready to send), plus 5 optional questions taken from the call script.
  5. Your reply, within one business dayA 2–3 minute screen recording of their demo, set to their farm type, and a setup checklist on WhatsApp.
  6. Setup, then pilotTwin live in about two weeks. Feedback through a 2-minute form or a WhatsApp voice note every two weeks.

Cal.com then becomes optional. Keep the call script as the source for the 5 async questions; the "weekly use" question becomes "How often would you open this?" with five choices.

How you'll know it works without calls

40%of farms you message open their demo (demo_opened with UTM tags)
50%of those touch it (demo_interaction)
5requests from about 22 farms (request_sent, plus the new request_step)
2pilots: the same goal as before, now reached through WhatsApp

These are starting targets for the first round, not benchmarks. Adjust them after the first 10 messages.
