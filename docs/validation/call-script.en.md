# Call script (English)

> **Not used for calls (decision, 8 October 2026).** The questions in section 2 and the scorecard are the source for the short written questionnaire in the no-call flow. Keep this page as a reference for what to learn from each farm.

A 30-minute call. The goal is not to sell. It's to learn whether this farm would open Canopy **every week**, and if so, to offer a free pilot.

**The one rule:** ask about what they did last week, not what they would do. "Would you use this?" always gets a polite yes. "How did you find out about the last crop problem?" gets the truth.

## Before the call (5 minutes)

- Open their personalised demo link from the outreach sheet and click around once, so nothing surprises you.
- Read their website or Instagram: crops, farm type, number of sites, who they sell to.
- Have the scorecard (bottom of this page) open to fill in straight after.
- Ask permission to take notes. Don't record unless they agree.

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)

> Thanks for making the time, {name}. I'll keep it to 30 minutes.
>
> Quick context: I'm building a live 3D view of indoor farms, every floor, crop and crate in one place. I'm not here to sell you anything today. I'd like to understand how you run {farm} now, and then show you what I've built so you can tell me honestly whether it's useful.
>
> Is it OK if I take notes?

## 2. Their farm today (10 minutes)

Pick the questions that fit. Listen more than you talk, and ask "can you give me an example?" whenever they generalise.

1. Walk me through {farm}: how big is it, what do you grow, how many people work there?
2. What does a normal week look like for you personally?
3. When you're not at the farm, how do you know what's happening there?
4. What do you track today: climate, water, lights, growth, harvests, orders? Where does it live (software, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, memory)?
5. Who looks at that information, and how often?
6. Tell me about the last time something went wrong with a crop. How did you find out, and how long did it take?
7. What did that cost you: lost crop, wasted time, an unhappy customer?
8. How do you plan harvests and deliveries today?
9. Do you ever need to show the farm to someone who isn't there: investors, buyers, a partner, a government programme? How do you do that now?
10. Have you tried any software or dashboards for this? What happened?

**Listen for:**

- Pain they've **already** spent time or money on. That's a strong signal.
- "I check it on my phone every morning" or "my manager sends me photos". That's a weekly habit we can replace.
- Several sites, or an owner who is often away. They need a single view.

## 3. The demo (7 minutes)

Share your screen with their personalised demo link (their farm's name is already on it).

1. **Floors:** "Here's the whole farm. Click a floor to zoom in: air, CO₂, water and lights sit where the sensors are."
2. **Light recipes:** "You can compare light recipes per floor and see the effect on growth speed and energy per kilo."
3. **Seed to plate:** "Each batch is followed through harvest, packing and delivery, with a log of what happened and when."
4. **Day and night:** "Drag the clock: see when the lights run and how much solar covers."

Then stop and ask:

- "What's the first thing you'd look at if this were your real farm?"
- "What's missing for you to open it every week?"
- "Who on your team would use it most?"

Don't defend the product. Write down every "but…".

## 4. The pilot offer (3 minutes)

Only offer it if they showed real pain or a weekly habit in step 2.

> Here's what I'm offering a small group of farms:
>
> - We build the twin of {farm} with you. Setup takes about two weeks after we have your layout and data, even if it's a spreadsheet.
> - Then you use it **free for three months**.
> - In return, a 20-minute feedback call every two weeks, and permission to mention you as a pilot farm. That can be anonymous if you prefer.
> - Your data stays yours. We don't share or sell it.
>
> Would that be worth trying at {farm}?

## 5. Close (3 minutes)

- **If yes:** "Great. The next step is a 45-minute session to map your rooms, floors and racks. Does [day] or [day] work?" Book it before you hang up.
- **If maybe:** "What would you need to see or know to decide?" Agree a date to follow up.
- **If no:** "Thanks, that's really useful. Who else do you know who runs an indoor farm and might find this interesting?"

Always end with: "Can I message you on WhatsApp if I have one more question?"

## Common objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already have software for the sensors." | "Great, that helps. Which one? Canopy sits on top of what you have and puts it in one view with the crops and deliveries. What's hard to see in your current tool?" |
| "We're too small for this." | "Small farms are who I'm building it for first. What takes you the most time each week?" |
| "Our data is private." | "Understood. It's your data. It's never shared or sold, and you can ask us to delete it any time." |
| "What will it cost after the pilot?" | "We'll set the price with pilot farms, based on what it's worth to you. The pilot is free and there's no commitment after it. What would it need to save you to be worth paying for?" |
| "I don't have time to set it up." | "We do the setup. We need about two hours of your time in total, spread over two weeks." |
| "Send me something by email." | "Happy to. So I send the right thing: what's the one question you'd want it to answer?" |
