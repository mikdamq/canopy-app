# Landing page images: brief and prompts

The owner makes these himself with an AI image tool (10 Oct 2026). Before choosing a tool, check it against the no-Israeli-products rule. Send the files to Claude in chat; Claude converts them to WebP, sizes them for phone and desktop, and places them.

## The look (same for every image)
- Photo-real, warm light: late afternoon sun or warm LED glow. Soft depth of field.
- Calm and uncluttered, with plenty of quiet space, because text and the 3D farm sit on top.
- Brand colours appear naturally: deep green leaves, a touch of magenta-pink LED light, cool off-white surfaces.
- No text, logos, watermarks or brand names. No recognisable faces. If people appear, show hands or backs only.
- The region: Jordan and the Gulf (desert light, stone, olive trees, date palms). Not European fields.

Add this line to the end of every prompt:
> photorealistic, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, calm composition with empty space, no text, no logos, no watermark, no faces

## How to send them
- Highest quality the tool gives (PNG or JPG), at least the size above.
- Make 2–3 versions of each and send the best one or two; Claude will say which fits best.
- Name them by number, e.g. `1-hero-desktop.png`.
